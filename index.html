About the data and design

This visualization uses the supplied NOAA daily weather dataset for 2017. Each location is a weather station in a U.S. state or territory. Daily midpoint temperature is calculated as (TMIN + TMAX) / 2, since reported TAVG is often missing. Canadian stations are excluded.

Stability is measured by the standard deviation of daily midpoint temperatures. A lower value means temperatures stayed closer to their period average. Stations need observations for at least 80% of available dates in the selected period. Partial seasons should not be interpreted as full-season results, and a single year does not describe long-term climate.

Horizontal bars make station rankings easy to compare and provide room for station names. Every bar starts at zero; the scale stays the same when switching seasons. The season dropdown is a dynamic filter, and tooltips provide details on demand. A heatmap and daily chart were considered, then removed to keep the visualization focused on the ranking question.

Source: CMSC 471 Assignment 1, supplied weather.csv (NOAA daily weather observations). The code structure, transitions, and tooltip interaction draw on our Lab 4 D3 visualization. The chart is implemented with D3.js (version 7.9.0).
